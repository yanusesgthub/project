## In just a sentence or two, summarize your project. (E.g., "A website that lets you buy and sell stocks.")

An arcade style game where you flip gravity and elimate enemies

## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

The game is heavily inspired by g-force and geometry dash but with an arcade aspect to it. Your character runs automatically and you press a button to flip from the floor to the ceiling. You’ll have 3 lives and you have to shoot at incoming targets while running. There is no finish line and The game keeps scrolling and getting faster until you lose all 3 lives, and your score is based on how long you survive. It will be executed using godot.
